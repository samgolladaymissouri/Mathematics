This was a first graduate course in measure theoretic real analysis. I took this course Spring 2026, during my senior year at the Barstow School, as a visiting student at the University of Missouri - Kansas City. The course was taught by John Sawatzky using Sheldon Axler's Measure, Integration & Real Analysis, covering up to and including Lp spaces. 

My final project for this course was a (approximately) 40 minute presentation constructing the Haar Measure on Locally Compact Hausdorff Topological Groups.
